# Module Five: Bridge & Urban Infrastructure Intelligence

## 1. Overview
Located in `data/infrastructure/bridges/`, this module maintains structured engineering classifications and corridor proposals to address chronic grade conflicts and bottlenecks in **Mumbai**, **Nagpur**, and **Pune**.

---

## 2. Bridge & Grade Separation Categories

1. **Grade-Separated Flyovers**: Dual-carriageway direct pass-through with surface level slip-roads for local turning movements.
2. **Grade-Separated Interchanges**: Partial cloverleaf or multi-level directional flyovers for intersecting expressways.
3. **Road Overbridges (ROB)**: Elevated spans clearing railway electrification (OHE) clearance.
4. **Road Underbridges (RUB)**: Depressed sub-surface vehicular underpasses for dense urban heritage or airport zones.
5. **Elevated Expressway Corridors**: Multi-kilometer segmental viaducts bypassing dozens of surface traffic signals.
6. **Cable-Stayed Bridges**: Signature long-span maritime and river crossings.
7. **Pedestrian Foot Overbridges (FOB)**: Isolates pedestrian traffic from vehicular flow with lift access.
8. **Twin-Tube Bored Road Tunnels**: Subterranean expressway tunnels for hill and coastal bypasses.

---

## 3. Proposal Generation Heuristics

For recurring chronic bottlenecks (delay $> 30$ mins daily, speed ratio $< 0.30$):
- Evaluates surface conflict type (e.g. 5-arm intersection, railway level crossing, expressway merge).
- Compares recommended infrastructure against alternative options (e.g. why a flyover is preferred over an underpass due to high water table).
- Outlines preliminary capital cost categories, land acquisition scope, and geotechnical feasibility prerequisites.

> **Engineering Disclaimer**: Proposals generated by this module are preliminary urban planning aids. They do not substitute for certified geotechnical, structural, and environmental engineering DPRs.
